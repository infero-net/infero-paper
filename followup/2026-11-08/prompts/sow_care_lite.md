# [Sow Care Handbook (lite)](https://example.org/sow-care)

**Housing:**
*   **Gestation:** Group pens, 2.0 m² per sow, dry solid flooring.
*   **Farrowing:** Move in 5 days before due date; crate with creep area.

**Daily Routine:** Feed → Water check → Clean pen → Observe sows → Record → ...

**Goal:** Healthy sows, large litters, low piglet mortality.

**Nutrition:**
*   **Gestation:** 2.0–2.5 kg feed/day, adjust to body condition.
*   **Lactation:** Feed to appetite, up to 7 kg/day; water 30 L/day.
*   **Weaning:** Restrict feed the day of weaning, then flush for mating.
*   **Fiber:** Add straw or beet pulp to reduce constipation.

**Farrowing:** Room at 20 °C, creep lamp at 30 °C for piglets.

**Hygiene:** Wash and disinfect pens between batches; dry for 2–3 days.

*Cura suis, salus gregis.* Care for the sow, health of the herd.
